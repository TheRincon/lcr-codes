channel uses regardless of rate, so per-symbol is the comparable axis.

## What §8.2's unequal protection assumes

§8.2 pairs each ECC level's luma rate with a chroma rate two steps stronger, and calls the
inequality load-bearing: chroma is "measured over a noisier, lower-bandwidth channel".
That pairing is a quantitative claim — the extra protection is worth a specific number of dB,
and it is only the right amount if the chroma channel is worse by that same amount:

| level | luma rate | chroma rate | chroma margin |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 0.85 | 0.65 | 2.4 dB |
| 1 | 0.75 | 0.55 | 2.1 dB |
| 2 | 0.65 | 0.45 | 1.9 dB |
| 3 | 0.55 | 0.35 | 1.8 dB |

So the table as pinned assumes the chroma channel runs that many dB below luma. Confirming
or correcting it needs a measured chroma-channel SNR from the symbol path, which the decoder
does not yet report — `Diagnostics::estimated_snr_db` is luma only. That measurement is the
remaining work on open decision #5; the thresholds above are the half that can be settled
without optics.
